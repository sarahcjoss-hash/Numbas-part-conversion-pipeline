answer:  Slow twitch fibres are dark red because they have a high concentration of myoglobin which stores oxygen for aerobic respiration. Fast twitch fibres are pale pink because they contain low concentrations of myoglobin as they mainly rely on anaerobic form of respiration (creatine phosphate and lactic acid metabolism).
